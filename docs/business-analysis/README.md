# Business Analysis

**Responsible Group Member:** Xavier Ajike

## Overview

The Bristol Regional Food Network (BRFN) is a proposed digital marketplace connecting local food producers with customers.

Our business analysis looks at the main activities involved in running the marketplace and how different responsibilities are organised.

## Main Business Areas

### 1. Product and Inventory Management

This covers product listings and validation, stock updates, and seasonal availability.

### 2. Marketplace Operations

This covers customer purchasing, multi-vendor order coordination, payments, and producer settlements.

### 3. Quality Assurance and Community Services

This covers food quality, product traceability, sustainability, and community engagement.

## Relationship to Our BPMN

These three areas form the main lanes in our strategic BPMN model.

The strategic model covers the wider BRFN enterprise, while our selected use case focuses on product listings and inventory management.
